Matching avatar cropper

Everything happens in your browser, nothing is uploaded. Place two square crops on one picture to make a matching pair of Discord profile pictures. The dashed circle shows what Discord will display.

Drop an image here, paste one, or choose a file.

Wide images work best: each crop starts on one half.

Drag inside a square to move it, drag a corner to resize and drag the round handle near its top edge to rotate (hold Shift to snap to 15°). With the image selected, ← → ↑ ↓ nudge the active crop (hold Shift for bigger steps), + and − resize it, [ and ] rotate it by 1° (15° with Shift), and Tab on the buttons above switches crops.
